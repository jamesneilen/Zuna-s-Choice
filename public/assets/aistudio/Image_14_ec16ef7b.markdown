## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a joyful balance between authentic Cameroonian agricultural richness and modern direct-to-consumer nutritional authority. It serves parents, caregivers, and pediatric wellness advocates seeking nutrient-dense infant feeding (Weanimix) without compromise. The visual tone must evoke absolute maternal safety, wholesome organic purity, and child-centered joy.

The aesthetic philosophy unites **Tactile Organic Warmth** with **Premium Direct-to-Consumer Editorial Clarity**. Visual compositions avoid clinical coldness through sunny, sun-drenched pastoral backdrops, cloud-sculpted contours, gentle organic pill shapes, and textural cereal grain motifs. Interfaces are bright, expansive, and welcoming, grounding playful optimism with crisp editorial hierarchy and medical-grade nutritional transparency.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional balance between natural earthiness, pediatric friendliness, and high-conversion clarity:

- **Sky Blue (`#60C3F2`)**: Primary brand hero tone; drives optimistic hero headers, dynamic atmospheric bands, and celebratory visual accents.
- **Brand Purple (`#810778`)**: Expressive typography accent and focal marker; grounds titles with premium distinctiveness, editorial gravitas, and playful vibrancy.
- **Natural Green (`#087C21`)**: Primary conversion engine and certification marker; governs primary interactive triggers (CTAs), nutritional assurance badges, and organic harvest cues.
- **Warm Orange (`#F19A24`)**: Warm energy tone; used strictly for star ratings, dynamic sale pills, allergen notices, nutritional value highlights, and radiant badge accents.
- **Soft Blue (`#EAF8FF`)**: Foundation for alternating page sections, soft card panels, and container borders to maintain breathable, calm layering without visual fatigue.
- **Clean White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary content base, card foreground, and floating container surface.
- **Dark Charcoal (`#263238`)**: Foundational body color ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against white and soft-blue backdrops.

## Typography

Typography balances rounded friendliness with rigorous legibility. Using **Nunito Sans** across headlines, labels, and running body creates a cohesive harmony:
- ExtraBold (800) weights bring out the soft, organic qualities of letter terminals, delivering a welcoming, childlike cheerfulness without sacrificing premium polish.
- Regular (400) and SemiBold (600) weights provide high legibility for critical ingredient tables, dietary ratios, preparation instructions, and nutritional disclosures.
- Brand Purple (`#810778`) should be used for display headlines, primary headers, and pull quotes to establish a signature editorial presence.
- Dark Charcoal (`#263238`) is used for all body text layers to maintain accessible reading contrast.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an open 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous vertical air, preventing maternal decision fatigue and highlighting natural ingredient sourcing.

- **Desktop (>=1024px)**: 12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, centered container with `margin: 3rem` outer frame and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

Rhythmic spacing between major sections relies on deep vertical strides (`4rem` to `6rem`), often bounded by gently curved SVG cloud separators or rolling hill divider paths (`border-radius: 50% 50% 0 0 / 24px 24px 0 0`), mirroring Cameroonian agricultural landscapes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop shadows and stark grey edges, using sunlit ambient diffusion and organic surface nesting instead:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Section canvases in `#FFFFFF` and alternating `#EAF8FF` with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Cards & Lists)**: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(96, 195, 242, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(38, 50, 56, 0.04);` with a soft border (`1px solid #EAF8FF`). Gives cards a pillowy, weightless lift.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Surfaces & Hover States)**: `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(8, 124, 33, 0.14), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(38, 50, 56, 0.05);`. Used when hovering product bundles and highlighting shopping bags.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Cart)**: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(129, 7, 120, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(38, 50, 56, 0.06);`. Tints with faint Brand Purple to add comforting depth.

## Shapes

The design uses a Pill-shaped (`roundedness: 3`) design language, echoing baby cereals, rounded grains, smooth pebbles, and infant-safe tableware:

- Primary buttons, search inputs, tags, and category filters are rendered with pure capsule contours (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- Feature cards, nutritional panels, and recipe modules use `rounded-3xl` (`2rem` / `32px`).
- Minor component blocks (quantity steppers, tooltips, alert boxes) use `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`).
- Hero image masks use organic asymmetrical pebble corners (e.g., `32px 64px 32px 64px`) to reinforce natural, unrefined harvest values.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart, Buy Now)**: Pill shaped (`rounded-full`), Natural Green (`#087C21`) background, Clean White typography, bold label tracking. Hover state shifts to a deeper forest green with a gentle transform (`scale(1.02)`), elevated by a tinted ambient green shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Learn More, Ingredient Sourcing)**: White pill surface, 2px solid border in Sky Blue (`#60C3F2`), text in Brand Purple (`#810778`). Hover state fills surface with Soft Blue (`#EAF8FF`).
- **Tertiary/Ghost**: Brand Purple typography with rounded underline or icon arrow pill accent.

### Chips & Badges
- **Organic & Origin Badges**: Full pill (`rounded-full`), Soft Blue or pale green tint surface, Natural Green text, prepended by a botanical grain or leaf icon.
- **Nutritional Micro-Pill**: Warm Orange (`#F19A24`) solid surface with white bold text for callouts like "Rich in Iron", "No Refined Sugar", or "Local Cameroonian Grains".

### Cards
- **Product & Weanimix Showcase Cards**: Crisp white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-3xl` (2rem), surrounded by an ultra-soft border of `#EAF8FF`. Images sit atop soft sky-blue or pastel-grain backdrops. Bottom padding leaves room for dual price and green pill action buttons.

### Inputs & Quantity Controls
- **Form Controls**: Full pill curvature, background `#FFFFFF`, bordered in `1.5px solid #EAF8FF`, text in Dark Charcoal. Focus state triggers a 2px outline in Sky Blue (`#60C3F2`) with zero hard drop shadow.
- **Quantity Adjuster**: Compact capsule with soft-blue background, using Brand Purple iconography for plus and minus buttons.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Fully rounded (`rounded-full` for radios, `rounded-lg` for checkboxes), 2px solid outline in Sky Blue, filling with Natural Green upon selection, accompanied by a clean white checkmark.

### Nutritional & Age Milestones (Specialty Components)
- **Age-Stage Toggle**: Segmented pill dock containing sliding capsule indicators (e.g., "6+ Months", "9+ Months", "12+ Months") with soft transitions.
- **Sourcing Guarantee Strip**: Wavy, organic container featuring illustrated local grain icons (sorghum, maize, soybeans) and certified maternal testing endorsements.
